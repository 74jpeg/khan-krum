Krum’s khanate

During khan Krum's reign, the First Bulgarian Empire doubled in size - reaching regions from the middle Dunabe to the Dneiper, and from Odrin to the Tatra Mt. He forced 3 byzantine emperors to give up the throne during his reign.

How to play: watch the clips, then drag the cards (or use the arrows) into the order the events happened, and press Check order. First few winners get a prize!
